What stays fixed regardless of direction

Selection outline plus eight handles, hover outline, key hints on tools, a mode indicator, a zoom readout, and a dirty dot. Those come from the plan, not the theme. The theme decides colour, radius, whether panels float or dock, whether the grid is dots, lines or nothing, and how loud the accent is.
